Most organizations are moved in 2–4 weeks, and both systems stay live in parallel until every total reconciles. You choose the cutover date around your fundraising calendar — for most teams that means after year-end appeals and before spring events.

No. You turn engines on when you're ready for them. Many teams start with Growth + Relationship (website, giving, CRM) and add Execution (HR) and Revenue (finance) later.

Everything moves — donors, giving history, pledges, recurring gifts, event records and volunteers — and our team reconciles every total against your current system before cutover.
